# Product–Website Fit Rubric

Use this rubric before the general visual audit.

## Product Truth

Establish the product category, audience, job to be done, actual capabilities, maturity, conversion model, differentiators, and trust requirements. Mark each as verified, inferred, or unknown.

## Fit Tests

### Identity

- Can a new visitor name the product category after the hero?
- Does the site describe the implemented product rather than an aspirational platform?
- Are the product name, feature names, and core nouns consistent?

### Audience

- Does the page speak to the real user or buyer?
- Is the assumed sophistication appropriate?
- Are examples, proof, and objections relevant to that audience?

### Problem and Value

- Is the problem concrete enough to recognize?
- Is the promised outcome causally connected to the product?
- Does the site distinguish the product from the default alternative?

### Conversion

- Does the primary CTA match the product motion: try, buy, book, join, contact, or learn?
- Does the visitor have enough information and trust to take that step?
- Are competing calls to action subordinate?

### Product Evidence

- Do screenshots represent real states and plausible data?
- Are important claims demonstrated, sourced, or qualified?
- Does the repository support claims that can reasonably be checked there?

### Tone and Visual Language

- Does the tone match the cost, risk, and emotional context?
- Does the visual system help explain the product or merely signal “modern technology”?
- Does the site look appropriately mature without pretending to have more traction or capability?

## Mismatch Severity

- Critical: the site sells a materially different product or makes an unsupported high-risk claim.
- High: the target user, main value, or conversion action is substantially obscured.
- Medium: the product is recoverable but requires inference or unnecessary reading.
- Low: the site is accurate but could express the product more specifically.

## Substitution Test

Replace the product name with an unrelated SaaS or AI company. If the hero, benefit, testimonial, or CTA still reads naturally, identify the missing product-specific noun, mechanism, proof, constraint, or outcome.
