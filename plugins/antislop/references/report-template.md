# Antislop Review Template

## Verdict

State in two to four sentences whether the site represents the product accurately, where it loses credibility or specificity, and the single highest-impact correction.

## Scope

This is a presentation review. Do not turn missing backend behavior into a
finding unless it creates a visible defect in the rendered experience.

- Live URL and date reviewed
- Repository and revision when known
- Routes, viewports, and states inspected
- Important limitations
